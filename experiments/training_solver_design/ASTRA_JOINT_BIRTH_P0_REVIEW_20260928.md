# Joint SAD birth competition: bounded opportunity proposal

2026-09-28. Read-only source/literature audit and a CPU row-identity counterexample. No GPU experiment, training modification, or quality result was produced. This is a proposal for root review, not an instruction to launch. Neither novelty nor benefit is established.

## What the current evidence does and does not say

The P16-resolution SAD original-loss triad reports room `31.137257/.923742/.275955`, bicycle `24.623657/.751522/.249722`, garden `26.509658/.841572/.145261` (PSNR/SSIM/VGG-LPIPS). Removing the extra L2 term did not close its PSNR deficit. The separate native SAD curves gain only `.170381/.084273/.151596 dB` over their last 600 updates in room/bicycle/garden. These are developed held-out observations, not labels for selecting the proposed intervention. They do not establish a split-induced training-view failure.

SAD's own [paper §4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.28016v1) explicitly says its frequency metric evaluates each primitive separately, ignoring frequency capacity already provided by overlapping primitives. It also reports having tried progressive edge-count splitting, which converged more slowly. Therefore neither overlap awareness nor gradual edge-based splitting is a new contribution by itself.

The narrow hypothesis is: **some simultaneously selected, overlapping parent families have individually useful births but antagonistic joint births; committing one family's complete native split and leaving its competing parent unsplit can improve original full-image loss while reducing subsequent accepted work.** This is different from claiming expensive parents are useless, retaining an extra parent alongside all children, or fitting a global child-opacity correction.

For two parent families A/B and otherwise identical scene state, measure all four legal scenes `00`, `10`, `01`, `11`. Let `L_ab` be the complete original photometric loss. The interaction `I=L_11-L_10-L_01+L_00` detects non-additivity; positive I alone does not establish useful competition. Require a singleton to beat both `11` and the appropriate controls on unused training cameras, then validate the assembled set. No image-space interpolation or sum of local gains is admissible as the actual result.

## Source isolation and an independent implementation defect

Pinned source: [LinjieLyu/SADGS@029814fb5ed773e4a794cec5115951a64f610ab8](https://github.com/LinjieLyu/SADGS/tree/029814fb5ed773e4a794cec5115951a64f610ab8).

`train.py:321-374` constructs the structural selection and calls `densify_and_prune_structgs`. The latter executes clone at `gaussian_model.py:1007`, analytic split at `1011`, opacity/size/custom pruning at `1014-1044`, then caps all opacities at .8 and replaces the opacity optimizer tensor at `1046-1048`. The analytic split itself accepts an explicit mask, copies SH/opacity, creates the deterministic native grid and scales, appends children, then removes parents at `638-831`.

**The analytic split can be isolated at its entry/exit.** Capture the state immediately after clone and before line 1011, including the already-computed split mask and eta. Hold all non-experimental families' native split choices fixed. Use the exact function with a masked subset; stop immediately on its return, before custom pruning and opacity cap. No-split and all-split controls must start from the same captured state. A PLY alone is insufficient for any continuation: clone/model tensors, both optimizer histories, temporary radii, all accumulators, current gradients, RNG states, camera stack and iteration/LR clocks must be preserved.

**Confirmed index-operation defect, unmeasured real-scene incidence:** `full_prune_mask` uses pre-split row identities. Parent deletion compacts all model rows, but lines 1021-1027 only pad this local mask with false values; `prune_points` cannot update this external local variable. CPU counterexample following the exact concatenate/delete/pad order:

```
old IDs:          p0 p1 p2 p3 p4 p5
split parents:       p1       p4   (two children each)
post-split IDs:    p0 p2 p3 p5 p1.c0 p1.c1 p4.c0 p4.c1
old custom mask:        ^           (old index 2 targets p2)
padded mask deletes post-split index 2 = p3, not p2.
```

Thus a true old prune bit after a removed parent can target a different survivor, or even a new child. No split, all-false custom mask, or unaffected early indices can avoid the problem. Opacity/size masks are recomputed after the edit and are a separate operation. Normal calls really pass `custom_prune_mask` (`train.py:353,371`); incidence and visual effect in P16 are not known. Correct implementation transports stable old IDs / a keep-index mapping, then marks newly created children false under the existing non-inheritance convention. This repair is baseline correctness work, not an original mechanism or a demonstrated speed gain. Root should decide its separate read-only incidence/repair gate before full-event continuation. The isolated split probe avoids this downstream confound.

## Minimal P0, frozen before any quality inspection

Use the healthy P16 common data and original-loss SAD recipe, at native full effective resolution. Development scenes are **room and healed garden**, with bicycle reserved for later transfer. Do not reuse corrupted garden states. Capture the **1500 normal densification event** from an existing valid optimizer-complete trace; if none exists, one instrumented baseline prefix is required and its wall is recorded. No choosing the event after looking at losses.

Select 12 training cameras by indices `round(k*(Ntrain-1)/11)`, k=0..11, after sorting `image_name`; even k form six fit cameras A, odd k form six internal-check cameras B unused in the P0 edit selector. They remain training cameras and have already affected the 1500-step trajectory; B is not a trajectory-held-out generalization test. No official held-out GT is loaded in P0.

1. **Candidate census, before edits.** Candidate parents must be true structural-mask selections, actual combined split selections, and produce at least two children. For isolation exclude parents that qualify only through absgrad. Build spatial nearest-neighbor proposals among these parents (at most eight neighbors/parent). A pair must have projected support-ellipse bbox overlap of at least .25 of the smaller bbox in at least three of six A cameras, and camera-depth separation at most twice the larger projected depth standard deviation there. These are only proposal filters, not proof of common surface or redundancy. Rank by summed predicted child bbox area times bbox overlap; tie by old IDs. Greedily choose disjoint pairs, capped at **128 pairs and 32,768 proposed children**. Freeze pair IDs and all masks before computing photometric scores. If fewer than 16 pairs survive, stop this bounded configuration.

2. **Child-work screen first.** Render the native all-split state on A through the author native `get_flag=True` counter with an all-one metric map. Sum accepted Gaussian-pixel counts for children of selected pairs. If their pooled share is below **10% of the whole all-split scene**, stop this prespecified quota because its direct removable child work is small. This is a screening heuristic, **not a mathematical upper bound on net scene work**: a retained parent may change transmittance and alter accepted work for later Gaussians in either direction. Never interpret point count, bbox area, or tile count as accepted work. This quota-based failure would close this bounded proposal, not all overlap competition.

3. **Four exact finite states per pair.** All other pairs and all non-pool selected families remain at their author split-hook all-split choice. Rebuild `00/10/01/11` from the same split-entry capture; use the same grid, SH/opacity initialization and child state as author code. Render complete A images and evaluate `0.8 L1+0.2*(1-fusedSSIM)` over the full image. A deterministic selector may choose `10` or `01` only if its A mean beats `11` by more than the full-image native replay noise floor, and its marginal utility differs from the independent ranking derived from `00`. Otherwise keep `11`. No parameter fitting, opacity correction, continuous gates, or candidate-count sweep. Record all four losses and I. This is a costly offline oracle, not a deployable scorer.

4. **Assemble before judging.** Make one global scene from all chosen pair decisions; do not add independent pair gains. Compare on A and once on B against: native all-split; a count-matched independent singleton scorer based on `L_10-L_00` / `L_01-L_00`; a deterministic geometry-only quota control with the same retained child count; and no structural split for the pool. Keep all other scene state identical. The independent scorer is essential: if it performs equally, no specifically joint mechanism has been shown.

5. **Frozen immediate gates, both scenes separately.** On B require joint choice's mean original loss at least **0.5% lower than native all-split**, at least five of six B views not worse beyond replay noise, and mean loss better than the count-matched independent scorer beyond replay noise. Re-evaluate complete-image training PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS descriptively; do not choose decisions from B metrics. Also require **at least 10% fewer whole-scene accepted pairs** than all-split across B, and **at least 5% lower paired forward+original-loss+backward step time**, counting every additional runtime operation of this fixed scene. If any gate fails in either scene, stop without moving quotas, events, cameras or thresholds. These deliberately strict thresholds screen for a useful mechanism rather than tiny local wins.

Replay noise: run the identical no-edit scene three times on each A/B camera; require RGB/gradient parity under a frozen tolerance and use `3*max_pairwise_full_image_loss_difference` as the decision floor (never below 1e-7 absolute loss). Full original images, native ordering, alpha cutoff, early stop, and SSIM neighborhoods remain intact. Native counts must be taken after each complete intervention because rejected children can reveal additional background work.

Passing this fixed-state P0 **does not authorize full training**. Native children can be temporarily worse but valuable after adaptation; our old finite-edit failures demonstrate this danger. A separate root-reviewed short continuation gate would need a lawful full-event integration, matched topology/optimizer/RNG, a count-matched independent control, and unchanged original loss at every step. Do not resume past the isolated split hook with old gradients attached to newly replaced tensors. Choose continuation length before observing trajectories and stop if the immediate advantage does not survive. Production CUDA and full 30K are later gates.

**Staged execution after feasibility audit:** no optimizer-complete SAD 1500 state exists in the current room/garden artifacts, so stage 1 alone requires a fresh, instrumented, same-recipe prefix for each scene. Save the full split-entry capture and child-parent row map in an isolated output directory, then perform candidate census and the prespecified child-work screen. Stage 1 must stop before the four-state renders if either scene has fewer than 16 legal disjoint pairs or less than 10% selected-child accepted-work share. The all-split control is the state immediately after the analytic split hook, before subsequent pruning and opacity cap; it is not the complete native density event. At the 128-pair cap, the planned full P0 diagnostic needs approximately 6,348 full-resolution renders across two scenes (6,144 pair-state A renders, 72 no-edit noise renders, 12 cost-screen views and 120 assembled-arm views), excluding repeated paired step timing; record actual GPU time and do not call this a deployable method cost.

## Full wall path and prior-art boundary

The proposed saving must come from physically absent native child families, which removes their projection/sort/accepted raster-backward work and optimizer rows. It is not parameter sharing. Remaining unsplit parents may be broader, and changed transmittance may increase background work; therefore only net native counters and complete steps count.

For a deployable implementation the required inequality is:

`T_feature + T_overlap_graph + T_joint_score + T_validation + T_state_edit + T_repair + sum(T_new_steps) + T_output < T_baseline_full_process`.

Full-image per-pair renders in P0 are charged to the diagnostic budget and reported separately, never called free method overhead. If their cost is used by an actual trainer, it must repay within the remaining training horizon. A successful oracle still needs a measured cheap estimator that reproduces decisions on unused training states; no free-scoring speed claim is permitted. Additional longer fitting to fix quality consumes the same budget.

Distinctness is narrow and unproven: SAD already supplies early anisotropic grids, multiview consistency, and the explicit overlap limitation; FastGS supplies multiview importance, Taming supplies budgeting, and existing merging/pruning methods already remove redundancy. This proposal retains author primitive/grid semantics but measures **simultaneous competing births' exact non-additive loss** and selects whole families jointly. It differs from our old single finite-edit conditional score by testing birth–birth interactions against an independent same-count selector, rather than subtracting old-parameter repair space. However `TOPOLOGY_DESIGN.md` already warns about overlapping edits and joint validation, so merely implementing its general warning is not sufficient novelty. A source-level survey of joint sparsification/active-set selection remains mandatory before any original-method claim. If the independent same-count control ties, or if the rule reduces to ordinary overlap suppression, close the originality claim even if the implementation is faster.

## Newly checked and rejected adjacent direction

A learned one-sided opacity/support boundary is already covered directly by [3D-HGS](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.02720v4) §§3.2-3.4 (learned splitting plane, separate half opacities, half-aware bounds), [DisC-GS](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.15196v2) (projected Bezier boundaries and approximate boundary gradients), [3D Skew GS](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.18334v1), and [XClipGS](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.07760v1) (exact affine-EWA half-space integral). It is not an alternative original main direction.
