# Correlated-direction Gaussian birth: hypothesis and first gate

Date: 2026-09-28. **Proposed mechanism, not a validated optimization or novelty claim.** The purpose is to replace SAD's tensor-product child grid when its separate parent-axis frequency tests respond to the same observable image direction. It retains the original full-resolution `0.8 L1 + 0.2(1-SSIM)` at every optimization step.

## Concrete intervention

For a parent with covariance `Σ = L Lᵀ`, map local displacements to whitened coordinates `z=L⁻¹(x−μ)`. For each valid training camera `v`, form a symmetric positive-semidefinite directional-frequency matrix `H_v` such that the pinned SAD source's **instantaneous** eta for a unit direction `q` is `eta_v(q)=sqrt(qᵀ H_v q)`. In the projection branch this embeds the camera Jacobian and the sampled 2D structure tensor; in the wavelength branch it embeds the source's scalar maximum tensor eigenvalue and parent-axis length. The current source sets `weights_valid` to ones, so no extra transmittance weight is applied. Verify this single-view identity against the pinned implementation before using a new orientation.

SAD takes an elementwise maximum of eta over valid historical observations along its current parent axes, then makes `k_i=ceil(sqrt(max(eta_i,1)))`. A single 1500-step capture does **not** reconstruct that historical maximum, because geometry changed during training. For a fixed-state diagnostic, construct `Q` from eigenvectors of the sum of current-view `H_v` on prespecified training cameras, but count rotated children with the actual per-direction envelope `eta_i=max_v sqrt(q_iᵀ H_v q_i)` and `k_i=ceil(sqrt(max(eta_i,1)))`. Apply the same fresh-view envelope to the unrotated parent axes as the matched control. Do not use eigenvalues of the averaged matrix as `k_i`, and do not call the fresh-view control identical to the historical native event.

An off-diagonal correlation can make separate parent-axis tests request children along two or three axes even when one rotated direction explains the frequency. Build the deterministic child lattice in the **rotated whitened frame**. Axis `i` has `k_i` centers spaced `sqrt(12)/k_i` in whitened units and child covariance `1/k_i²` along that direction. Map offsets and covariances back by `LQ`. `k_i=1` leaves that direction unsplit inside each child. For an equally weighted normalized additive mixture, this construction preserves the parent's mean and covariance, just as SAD's native grid does; it does **not** promise equal alpha compositing, opacity, visibility, or image loss. Use the native initial SH and opacity only for a controlled first comparison; any optical-depth repair is a separate, costed intervention.

Illustrative possibility: if all valid views share `H_v=λ_v uuᵀ` with `u=(1,1,1)/sqrt(3)`, each matrix has three positive diagonal entries but one nonzero eigenvalue. A diagonal tensor-product rule may create children along all three parent axes, whereas the common eigenframe rule creates a one-dimensional family. This algebra does not show that real room/garden observations share a low-rank direction.

## Why this could meet quality and wall goals, and why it may fail

The candidate removes child families that only duplicate a correlated screen-space frequency direction, while allowing rotated children to resolve the actual direction. A smaller child set can remove projection/sort/raster-backward/optimizer work. It may also reduce volumetric floaters that harm unseen-view PSNR while preserving SAD's SSIM/LPIPS, but that quality effect is unproven. Multiple camera baselines may make `H` full rank; SAD §4.3 already found premature directional constraints harmed LPIPS. Fewer children can reduce representational capacity or change occlusion, and computing `H`, its eigensystem, and a new grid costs time. A valid result requires net accepted-hit and full-step savings after accounting for all additional work.

The fresh 1500-step original-loss SAD captures provide only a potential scale of the issue: room has 34,495 three-axis split parents yielding 497,246 of 993,750 children; healed garden has 136,786 such parents yielding 1,315,435 of 4,157,368 children. These are exact captured child counts, not accepted-hit shares, a proof of spectral low rank, or a quality result. Source: `OBSERVABLE_BIRTH_P0_20260928/{room,garden}_child_distribution.json` and remote split-entry captures. The older pairwise joint-birth proposal is NO-GO as an original algorithm; its captures are reused here only as frozen state evidence.

## Frozen first gate before CUDA or full training

Use the existing healthy room and garden 1500-step split-entry captures, the same sorted twelve training cameras as `ASTRA_JOINT_BIRTH_P0_REVIEW_20260928.md`, and no formal held-out GT. Reconstruct the exact SAD directional eta inputs from the captured training state and source data. First verify `sqrt(e_iᵀ H_v e_i)` against the pinned implementation's instantaneous per-axis eta on each sampled valid camera within a frozen numerical tolerance; document and stop on a genuine single-view formula mismatch. Historical `max_eta_3ch` need not equal this fresh-view calculation. Then report the distribution of summed-matrix eigenvalue ratios and off-diagonal correlations, matched fresh-view unrotated versus rotated `k` and child counts, and the historical native child counts separately. Stratify the child-count opportunity by actual native child accepted-hit counts on six fit cameras. Use six distinct internal-check training cameras to check the spectrum and child-count opportunity; they have participated in the training trajectory. Recheck every rotated child's complete projected covariance/frequency response to avoid dropping a mixed direction merely because an eigenvalue is small.

Do not launch a new-method training arm merely because children are fewer. A follow-on fixed-event renderer probe must build both complete legal split-hook scenes from the same entry state, evaluate full-image original loss on all twelve training cameras, and count net native accepted hits plus paired forward+loss+backward time. Stop if the rotated layout worsens original loss on the internal-check cameras or if total work does not decrease materially. Only after a positive fixed-event gate and source-level novelty audit should a matched short continuation test whether the effect survives learning; full multi-scene/multiseed training and FastGS-Big/ACE-GS comparisons remain required.

## Prior-art boundary

SAD §3.3 already computes direction-dependent projected frequency and §4.3 shows a negative directional ablation. BOGausS uses multiview projection precision, and SteepGS/EDC/ShorterSplatting address geometry and split cost. A scalar trace, changing one native `k_i`, or rotating by the parent covariance alone is not new. The only candidate difference here is preserving cross-axis frequency correlation through the *full* parent-whitened `H` and changing both the complete child offset lattice and child covariances to its eigenframe. This distinction still needs a bounded full-text/source-level prior-art check before calling it original.
