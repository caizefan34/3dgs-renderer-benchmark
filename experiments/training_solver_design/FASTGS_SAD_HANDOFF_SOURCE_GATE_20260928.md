# FastGS → SAD-GS PLY handoff: source and wall-clock gate

Date: 2026-09-28. Read-only source/log audit; no handoff training, quality evaluation, or code change was performed.

**Decision: do not launch a Luna GPU arm for the current two-stage recipe.** This is a combination of existing methods, not an identified original mechanism. The unchanged SAD-GS `--ply_path` route also freezes xyz: `GaussianModel.__init__` initializes `spatial_lr_scale=0`; `train.py` loads the PLY, constructs `Scene`, calls `training_setup(opt)`, and only then sets `spatial_lr_scale=scene.cameras_extent`. `training_setup` has already put a zero xyz learning rate into Adam and captured the zero scale in the exponential scheduler. Reassigning the model field afterward does not fix either value. A meaningful warm-start test would first set the scene extent before optimizer/scheduler setup and verify a nonzero xyz LR. This is an entry-path correctness issue, not a new algorithm.

FastGS and SAD-GS PLY layouts match for xyz, SH, scale, opacity and rotation at the same SH degree. SAD-GS supplies zero for absent `filter_3D`, and its `Scene` does not replace the loaded points. The PLY lacks Adam moments/step, random state, camera stack, and densification statistics. SAD-GS restarts its local clock, including bbox initialization, densification, opacity reset and pruning under its default config. A handoff would therefore be a cold-optimizer warm-start rather than a continuation of the FastGS optimization trajectory.

The timed FastGS from-zero room 30K process took 287.270 s, with 228.418 s in the author trainer loop and 58.852 s outside it (`FASTERGS_BASELINE_PROTOCOL.md`). Its tqdm log has about 93 s of loop time by 10K and 171 s by 20K; it has no process termination at those checkpoints. The mathematical remaining SAD budgets are therefore **less than 194.270 s** after 10K and **less than 116.270 s** after 20K, because each prefix needs startup/loading and a handoff save. Applying the measured 30K loop-external 58.852 s as a rough, **unmeasured prefix estimate** gives about 151.9 s to a 10K prefix and a remaining budget of about 135.4 s. Adding the independent SAD original-loss 3K process time of 140.937 s gives roughly 292.8 s before extra transfer cost; the 5K SAD process took 188.996 s. Prefix overhead could differ, so this is not an impossibility proof. A separate same-protocol FastGS parallel-loader run already has 248.50 s complete process wall (`PARALLEL_CAMERA_LOAD_RESULTS.md`), an even tighter practical comparison. None of these times are handoff measurements.

Existing room FastGS 10K/20K quality is 30.400068/.9136015/.2174812 and 32.020439/.9291240/.1912480 (PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS), below the final 30K model in different metrics. Independent SAD 3K/5K PSNR is also below the FastGS-Big 30K target. These endpoints do not predict the handoff trajectory or certify a three-metric gain.

If this is revisited as a baseline calibration, first test the corrected loader's xyz LR and schedule without training; then measure the true full-process prefix plus save/load and freeze the handoff iteration and SAD local schedule. Even a successful combination is not an originality claim.
