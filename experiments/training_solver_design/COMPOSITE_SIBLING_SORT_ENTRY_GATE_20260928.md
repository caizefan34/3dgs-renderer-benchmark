# Two-lobe sort-entry packing: order and cost gate

Date: 2026-09-28. Read-only algebra/source screen; no sorting export, GPU code, training or quality evaluation.

Proposal: pack two overlapping, high-utility Gaussians into one sort/intersection entry while retaining both internal opacity/color lobes and separate trainable parameters. This targets P15's high-utility/high-intersection population without freezing its updates.

**Decision: no Luna experiment on the current proposal.** Exactness requires the two lobes to be consecutive in the original depth order for each relevant tile/pixel, with their internal order preserved. If the original order is `A,X,B`, one packed entry can place `A,B` only before or after `X`; the color error from swapping `B` and `X` is generally `T_before * alpha_B * alpha_X * (color_B - color_X)`. Closeness in depth or overlapping footprints does not certify this condition. A previous 0.1σ child-motion screen found other Gaussians interleaving 67.6%–94.5% of same-tile child pairs (`TRANSMITTANCE_SPLIT_DESIGN.md`/`SURVIVAL_MOTION_RESULTS.md`), which is not a direct measurement of packing eligibility but makes a broad exact opportunity doubtful.

For exact union enumeration, the most sort/intersection entries one pair can save is `|T_A ∩ T_B|`, since the original count is `|T_A|+|T_B|` and the packed count is `|T_A ∪ T_B|`. Both Gaussian projections, SH evaluations, per-pixel alpha/color computations, independent parameter gradients and Adam states remain. FastGS's 32-Gaussian bucket would need variable lobe handling. Pair discovery/continuity certification, union construction and fallback costs must be less than the entry-generation/sort savings. No measured eligible-overlap or complete-step bound currently supports this. Exact packing could preserve image metrics, but it supplies no quality-improvement mechanism and no verified complete-process speedup.

Prior-art correction: an older workspace gap audit had called Splatshop a 2024 training-time Gaussian-merging method. The later source audit `reports/phase-c17-c2/c1_prior_art_differentiation.md` §3 identifies Schütz et al.'s 2025 interactive editor, which globally depth-sorts splats before constructing tile fragments; it does not establish training-time merging or this packed two-lobe primitive. The two copies of the older audit were corrected. This narrower boundary does not prove the packing idea original or useful.
