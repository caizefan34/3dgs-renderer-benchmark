    Abstract: Dynamic 3D Gaussian… ▽ More Dynamic 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) achieves photorealistic reconstruction of time-varying scenes, and recent physics-aware extensions improve extrapolation by explicitly predicting velocity fields. However, these extensions merely fit vector fields to visual deformations without satisfying Lagrangian mechanics, leading to three major issues: (i) physically inconsistent trajectories, (ii) lack of time-reversibility, and (iii) geometric collapse during long-term extrapolation. In this paper, we propose LagrangeGS, which formulates dynamic 3DGS as a non-conservative Lagrangian system.
